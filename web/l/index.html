Send the board's logs

Something went wrong on your CYD? Its logs tell the developer what happened. Nothing personal is in them: the board, the firmware, memory, restarts, errors and the games found on the card.

Get the logs

    
With a phone: on the board, open Settings → Logs → Send Logs and point your phone's camera at the QR code. This page opens with the logs.

    
With a computer: put the board's SD card in it. The logs are the text files in GOLDBOX/_CYD (ERRORS.TXT, MEMORY.TXT, MEMORY.OLD, RESTART.TXT, SCAN.TXT): attach them to an email.
